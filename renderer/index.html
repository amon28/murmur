Built with

  • llama.cpp runs the chat model · MIT
  • Gemma 4 by Google DeepMind, the chat model (QAT build by Unsloth)
  • whisper.cpp listens to your voice · MIT
  • Whisper by OpenAI, the speech model · MIT
  • EmbeddingGemma by Google, finds related notes and chats
  • Transformers.js runs it · Apache-2.0
  • Electron the app window · MIT
  • Playwright web search through Edge · Apache-2.0
  • unpdf and mammoth read PDF and Word files
